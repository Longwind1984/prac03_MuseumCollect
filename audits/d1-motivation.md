# Motivation Audit — Day 1 — "Why do I keep opening this?"

## My persona: 2-week veteran user, 30+ collected
## Demos reviewed: v1-A (考据派), v1-B (沉浸派), v1-C (探索派)

> Reviewer stance: I'm 2 weeks in. I've stopped reading every tooltip. I open the app on the train. The novelty animation is gone. I now ask: **does this make me smarter, or just keep score?**

---

## The big question per demo

### v1-A (考据派 / 青铜考)

**1. Why would I open this today?**
The "待补 · 研究断面建议" block on `me.html` is the only real return-trip hook. It tells me three specific holes in my knowledge ("您当前录入集中于商晚期。商早(二里岗)与商中暂为空白"; "您已覆盖范铸法 9 件,但失蜡法 0 件") with named candidate artifacts (子龙鼎 / 曾侯乙尊盘 / 青铜大立人). This is **substantive** — it's not "you have 7/25", it's "here is a structural gap in your understanding". Strong.

**2. Session goal?**
"Read up on / collect one of the candidates the suggestion panel pointed me at." That's a real session goal — finite, knowledge-shaped, with a clear "done" state.

**3. What's "almost there"?**
The 段位 bar ("距下段 — 字") is genuine — it ties to actual character knowledge, not collection count. The "海内三宝 2/3" achievement is delightful because the third (毛公鼎) is the cultural payoff. The "三家朝圣 X/14" is reasonable.

**4. What identity am I building?**
"主时代偏 商" + "夔龙派(隐式)" + "金文识读·二段" + "海内三宝集". Identity is **descriptive** ("based on what you actually did") not aspirational ("level up to look cool"). That's the right frame for this persona.

**5. Off-ramp?**
The "本系统对识别置信度采取诚实做法" tone carries through. No daily streaks, no red badges, no urgency text. The "夏 / 商早期史料稀少非用户之过" footnote on time-pillar.html is **actively kind** — it preemptively absolves user guilt for empty segments. This is the only demo that thought about off-ramping.

---

### v1-B (沉浸派 / 青·铜)

**1. Why would I open this today?**
"还在等你的 / 尚未唤醒" section on me.html, which lists 4 uncollected items with a poetic teaser ("它埋了三千年" / "去 听 它 说 →"). Plus "今日之国宝" on index.html. The hook is **emotional curiosity** ("what story will I get today?"), not progress. That's real but **thin** — by week 2, the poems start sounding interchangeable.

**2. Session goal?**
"Read one new artifact's monologue, listen to its 铭文之声 audio." Almost a literary-magazine reading session. Session length is unbounded (could be 90 seconds or 20 minutes). **This is actually one of B's strengths** — sessions don't feel like task-completion.

**3. What's "almost there"?**
On time-pillar.html: "还差 ${total - filled} 件,${seg.label}通鉴可得。" Decent. On ancient-map.html: "尚有 N 处待踏足". The 朝圣护照 / 流散篇 emotional hook ("本应在家。毛公鼎在台北...等一张回来的船票") is **the strongest emotional pull of any demo**. I'd come back specifically to read more "流散" stories.

**4. What identity am I building?**
"夔龙派初识 / 商之听者 / 古蜀见证 / 鼎之听者 / 国宝偏爱". The titles are poetic but **mostly tautological** — they restate what I already collected ("you have 2 ding so you're a 鼎之听者"). They don't tell me I'm becoming *better* at anything.

**5. Off-ramp?**
No daily mechanic, no streaks. The "等过三千年" framing is naturally chill — the artifact has been waiting; one more week won't hurt. **Best off-ramp tone of the three**, by accident or design.

---

### v1-C (探索派 / 青铜图鉴 · 探索版)

**1. Why would I open this today?**
The "NEXT MILESTONE / 再收 3 件 → 解锁'商代食器子图鉴'" panel on index.html. And the "即将解锁 / 再识 3 字 → 铭文识读·三段" card on me.html. These are crisp, gamified, milestone-shaped hooks. **For Day 1-7 this works.** For week 2+ veterans, **this is exactly the kind of cheap chase I'm tired of** — the "milestone" is just a number threshold, and "商代食器子图鉴" doesn't promise new knowledge, just a new sub-collection page.

**2. Session goal?**
"Get 3 more bronzes to unlock the next thing." Pure XP grind frame. The session feels like *progress on a meter*, not *learning*.

**3. What's "almost there"?**
**Everywhere.** 国宝学徒 70%, 三家朝圣者 67%, 尊器收藏家 75%, 通鉴 4/7, 朝代覆盖 57%, 三段还差 3 字. Five+ "X% complete" bars on one screen. This is **the textbook Skinner pattern** — saturated progress optics regardless of underlying meaning.

**4. What identity am I building?**
"青铜见习 · 第 4 周" + "国宝学徒" + "尊器收藏家" + "铭文识读·二段". These are **aspirational ranks** ("学徒 → 学者") and they're decoupled from real expertise — 国宝学徒 unlocks at "10 件国宝", not at "you can identify 10 国宝 unaided". Identity is **performance-of-collection**, not actual competence.

**5. Off-ramp?**
The "🌿 不必每天打开" banner is a paste-on disclaimer at the bottom of me.html. Meanwhile the rest of the page screams "你已 70%! 再 3 件! 即将解锁!". The disclaimer doesn't actually work because **the entire visual hierarchy contradicts it**. The "Anti-Skinner-box 承诺" on scan.html ("揭示动效 ≤ 2s 可跳过") is similarly a paste-on label rather than a design choice.

## Hook quality matrix (1-5)

| Hook type | v1-A | v1-B | v1-C |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Completion 完成感** | 4 — 待补 suggestions tie completion to specific knowledge gaps | 3 — "通鉴" stamps and "尚未唤醒" list, but no granular sub-period | 5 — saturated, but quality of each completion is shallow |
| **Connection 关联感** | 5 — pattern-tree, cross-ref, 同型对照墙, 同坑兄弟. Every artifact pulls 3 directions | 2 — most pages are one-deep; "穿越" era switch is connection-lite | 3 — "二重文明见证者" callout is nice; otherwise mostly hub-and-spoke |
| **Rarity 稀有感** | 3 — present but deliberately suppressed (国宝率, no gold glow on every element) | 4 — halo-treasure used selectively; 国之重器 seals carry weight | 4 — gold halos everywhere; risks inflation but reads as "premium" |
| **Story 故事解锁** | 4 — 段位 progress unlocks reading capability + 流散 narrative + 考古笔记 per site | 5 — 国宝独白, 国宝档案 5-段时光卷, 礼器归位 narrative ★ best in class | 2 — 梗 lines ("最重的网红") are funny once; no actual story arc |
| **Identity 身份认同** | 4 — 段位 is real (围棋 lineage); 主时代/纹饰派 derived from real data | 3 — "夔龙派初识" reads more as label than self-image | 3 — 学徒→学者 reads aspirational but content-thin |
| **Spatial-Temporal Completion** | 4 — time-pillar with historical event annotations + 古国 borders + 流散 篇 | 5 — 时间柱 + 古国"穿越"切换 makes 三星堆-殷墟 共时 viscerally real | 3 — time-pillar present but didactically tame; map present but lighter |

**Top-level**: A wins on Connection + Identity. B wins on Story + Spatial-Temporal. C wins on Completion but at quality cost.

## Where motivation is real

- **v1-A me.html 待补建议**: Three specifically-named knowledge gaps with named candidate artifacts. This is genuine "what should I learn next" not "what should I farm next".
- **v1-A artifact.html 同型对照墙**: 4-cell wall with the 4th slot as 朱砂虚线"待补" — this is collection-as-research, not collection-as-pokedex.
- **v1-A time-pillar.html right column "历史大事件"** annotations with citations (利簋甲子朝, 牧野之战 BC 1046.02.20). I open the app to learn one more thing about one more date.
- **v1-B ancient-map.html 流散篇 / 本应在家**: "毛公鼎在台北...等一张回来的船票" — this is the only place in any demo where my heart moved. It's *not* a hook in the gamification sense, it's a hook in the emotional-investment sense. Sustained.
- **v1-B purpose-scene.html 礼器归位**: The 22-position 太牢礼 scene with "天子九鼎八簋 / 诸侯七鼎六簋" tier-judging — this is **the only place across all 3 demos** where filling slots teaches a real fact (列鼎制度) that I actually didn't know before.
- **v1-C scan.html "DAY-1 WOW · 同时点亮"**: Three dimensions visually light up after scan. Smart Day-1 hook. But by Day 14, **I've stopped noticing**.

## Where motivation is faked (worse than nothing) ★

> The most important section. A bad gamification hook is worse than no hook because it teaches me to optimize for the wrong thing.

### v1-C **(the heaviest offender by far)**

1. **`me.html` stats grid (lines 60-92)** — 5 large numeric meters: 总收藏 / 国宝率 / 朝代覆盖 / 铭文识读 / 出土地. Visually this **is** an XP dashboard. The "国宝率 100% (行业 4%)" with full bar is a **vanity metric** — it's at 100% because the seed data hand-picked 7 国宝. By Week 2 I'd realize this number is meaningless.

2. **`me.html` "即将解锁 / 再识 3 字 → 铭文识读·三段" + "再收 3 件国宝 → 头像挂'宝'印章" + "尊器收藏家 已 3/4 · 再 1 件解锁"** — three "差 N 件 / 字 解锁 X" prompts visible at once. `gamification-mechanics.md §5.3` explicitly forbids this:
   > 永远不在 UI 上有"还有 3 件就达成"的红字 / 紧迫感

   **C violates this rule three times on its 我的 page.**

3. **`index.html` "NEXT MILESTONE / 再收 3 件 → 解锁'商代食器子图鉴'"** — The reward is *another collection page*. Unlocking pages is fake-content. Compare to A's "再录入大盂鼎可推进段位 — 该铭含您未识字若干" which is real-knowledge.

4. **`scan.html` "DAY-1 WOW · 同时点亮"** with three pulsing amber dots — this is *the slot machine ding*. Each scan flashes the same three dimensions regardless of content. By scan 30, this no longer means anything.

5. **`me.html` "下一步可解锁" section** with three progress bars at 70/67/75% — **all three at near-completion is suspicious by design**. Classic "always show progress just short of a reward" manipulation. The bars are calibrated, not earned.

6. **`me.html` 梗卡片 generator** — "梗" line + 国宝印章 + 称号水印. This is **social-flex shaped collection** ("我比国博还早收藏到 XX 鼎"). It rewards **sharing about collecting**, not **understanding what was collected**.

7. **`scan.html` "Anti-Skinner-box 承诺" footnote** — the demo *knows* it's flirting with Skinner-box territory and tries to inoculate against critique. The disclaimer doesn't undo the design; it confesses it.

### v1-B (selective offenses)

1. **`me.html` "苏念之卷 — 我等过三千年,你来看我"** opening — **as a 2-week veteran, this poem is the same poem I read on Day 1**. The system never advances the relationship; the 妇好鸮尊 keeps saying its one line. The "monologue" framework doesn't ladder.

2. **`me.html` "已得的称号" titles row** — 8+ titles at once including "踏 上 青 铜 之 路 · Day 1 · 永不褪色". Titles are inflated; "永不褪色" is the giveaway — it's a participation badge.

3. **`time-pillar.html` "通鉴 / 渐入 / 待踏足" seals** — three seal states, but pure visual reward decoupled from any new knowledge. The "通鉴" seal at ≥80% fill triggers on count, not on understanding.

4. **`me.html` 共享卡 "三千年的厚度,我已走过八件"** — boast-shaped. The card is for sharing collection count, not for sharing learning. (Same issue as C's 梗卡片 but less aggressive.)

### v1-A (minor offenses)

1. **`me.html` "金文识读 · 二段" stamp + 进度** — segment 段位 is good in principle, but **"二段(50字)→ 三段(150字)"** thresholds are arbitrary count gates. Is the user actually 3x better at identifying 金文 between 二段 and 三段? Not demonstrably. The 围棋 lineage gives it gravitas but the underlying mechanic is still "字数 累计 → tier".

2. **`me.html` 国宝率 = X%** — even with the "本系统不做排行榜" disclaimer, displaying a single percentage that you can compare to "行业基线 ≈ 4%" *is* leaderboard logic with two participants (you vs. average). Subtle.

3. **`me.html` "海内三宝集 X/3"** — the three artifacts (大盂鼎 / 大克鼎 / 毛公鼎) are real and meaningful, but the framing is checklist. Could be reframed: "您已对照三宝中 X 件的字体演变" (action-shaped, not check-shaped).

## Anti-Skinner compliance check (per `gamification-mechanics.md §3`)

The doc enumerates 9 forbidden patterns. Pass/fail per demo:

| # | Forbidden | v1-A | v1-B | v1-C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 每日签到积分 | ✅ none | ✅ none | ✅ none |
| 2 | 体力/疲劳值/等待 | ✅ none | ✅ none | ✅ none |
| 3 | 单纯排行榜 | ⚠ "行业基线 4%" implicit benchmark | ✅ none | ⚠ "国宝率 vs 行业 4%" repeated, more prominent |
| 4 | 普通徽章成就墙 | ⚠ "隐式研究称号" panel is technically a 成就墙 with 5+ rows | ⚠ "已得的称号" row is a 成就墙 with 8 titles | ❌ "待解锁称号" section with 3 progress bars is an **explicit 成就墙 + 进度** |
| 5 | 抽卡机/概率掉落 | ✅ none | ✅ none | ⚠ scan.html visually is "card drop / 翻面" — frames real recognition as a *pull*. Mechanically OK (no probability), visually borderline. |
| 6 | 限时活动/FOMO | ✅ none | ✅ none | ✅ none |
| 7 | 推送通知 | ✅ none | ✅ none | ✅ "不每日推送催" promise |
| 8 | 充值解锁 | ✅ none | ✅ none | ✅ none |
| 9 | XP 数值面板 | ⚠ headline metrics row (5 numbers) is XP-shaped but each number is meaningful | ✅ stats are narratively framed | ❌ index.html + me.html both have "X / Y" 4-stat panels in classic XP layout |

**Tally**: A ≈ 5 pass, 4 ⚠. B ≈ 7 pass, 2 ⚠. C ≈ 3 pass, 4 ⚠, 2 ❌.

**C is the only demo with explicit Anti-Skinner failures by the team's own spec.**

## The "becomes an expert" test

> After 30 artifacts, does the user actually know more 青铜器 than they would have by reading a single textbook?

**v1-A: Yes.** The 待补 system pushed me to learn about (a) 二里岗 vs 殷墟 distinction, (b) 失蜡法 emergence at 曾侯乙, (c) 三星堆 as independent non-中原 civilization. The 时空柱 left-column historical events (利簋"甲子朝"→ BC 1046.02.20) are knowledge bullets I'd carry. The 同型对照墙 forced me to compare 4 方尊. The 字体比较 (王字 X 形 → 楷化) is a real philological observation. **I leave A with knowledge I can use in conversation.**

**v1-B: Partially.** I'd remember **the 列鼎制度** from 礼器归位 (very memorable), the **流散史** stories per artifact (emotional sticky), and the **三星堆 与 殷墟 共时** awareness from 穿越 era switching. But I would *not* know how to identify a 夔龙纹 vs a 饕餮纹, would not know what 失蜡法 is, would not be able to read any 金文. **I leave B as a more passionate person but not a more knowledgeable one.**

**v1-C: Largely no.** I would know I have 7 国宝 at 100% rate. I would know my "段位" is 二段 (47 字). But the demo gives me **no path to actually identify those 47 characters off the app** — the 梗 lines have replaced the铭文释读. The 朝代 chips are filterable but not actively taught. The 形制谱系 is Pokédex-shaped (slots to fill) rather than knowledge-shaped (lineage to understand). **C teaches me how to share, not how to see.**

## Severity ranking

### [P0 motivation collapse]
- **C's stacked "差 N 件解锁" panels** (`me.html` "下一步可解锁" + index.html "NEXT MILESTONE" + me.html "即将解锁"). By Week 2, this stops feeling like progress and starts feeling like grinding. Once the spell breaks, the user notices the rewards are paper.
- **B's static 妇好鸮尊 monologue on me.html**. The artifact's "我等过三千年" is on the personal page **after 30 collections** — same line, same artifact, no relationship deepening. Reveals that B's emotional layer doesn't ladder past Day 3.
- **All three demos' 称号 systems are still labels-on-data, not data-on-skill**. None of them have a single称号 that requires demonstrated knowledge (e.g., "correctly identified 10 unknown 饕餮 vs 凤鸟 in a blind test" → 鉴定师). The team's vision in motivation-hooks §1.5 ("识别了 6/12 种夔龙变体") is **not implemented in any demo**.

### [P1 motivation leak]
- A's 段位 thresholds are still 字数 累计 gates (not "demonstrated reading of a passage"). The 围棋 framing gives gravitas but is paper-thin.
- B's "通鉴 / 渐入 / 待踏足" seals trigger on count percentages, not on demonstrated synthesis.
- C's 国宝率 100% (行业 4%) is a vanity number that will deflate as the catalog grows. The user will notice their 国宝率 dropping over time and feel punished for breadth.
- None of the demos implement the **跨维度组合** unlocks (e.g., "二重文明见证者" requires 商晚期 + 三星堆 collections). C has a static "已达成" callout for this on ancient-map.html but the unlock is not actually computed cross-dimensionally.
- None of the demos surface "your collection profile is shifting" diagnostics (e.g., "you used to focus 商代, now you're branching to 战国 — keep going?"). This is a missed identity-formation hook.

### [P2 missed motivation upside]
- **No demo implements 长铭 reading-as-story**. The 毛公鼎 499 字 → "解一篇文章 — 整段故事豁然" (motivation-hooks §1.8) is the highest-prestige expert hook and **the most defensible Skinner-immune mechanic**. None of the three demos let me start reading a long inscription and feel the story unfold.
- **No 朝代故事卷 unlock**. Time-pillar.html in A and B both lack the "click 朝代 → 时代故事卷" mechanism described in motivation-hooks §1.1.
- **No 拓片本 standalone collection** in C and B. Only A has 拓片放大镜 as a feature, but even there it's per-artifact, not a separate cross-artifact 拓片家族 collection.

## Bottom-line ranking (motivation only)

1. **v1-A (考据派)** — Sustainable motivation. Identity = competence. 待补 system gives **real session goals**. Anti-Skinner discipline is mostly internalized in the visual language (calm, no spinners, no urgency). Will retain hardcore users for 3-12 months without burnout. Risk: surface is too austere for soft users; might not get them through Day 1.

2. **v1-B (沉浸派)** — Strong emotional motivation **for the first few weeks**. 流散篇 + 礼器归位 are the most memorable single experiences across all demos. But the monologue framework doesn't ladder; static narratives stop deepening. Week-4 user will start noticing the same 8 poems repeating. Will lose users to "I've heard this app's stories already".

3. **v1-C (探索版)** — Strong Day-1 hook ("开盲盒 / 翻面 / 多维点亮"). Cheap and effective for first session. **By Day 14, the same patterns that hooked me on Day 1 actively bore or annoy me** — saturated progress bars, "即将解锁" prompts, and 梗 lines feel like ads for the next collection. The product teaches the user to collect for collection's sake. C will spike then crash on retention.

## The killer question

> "In 6 months, will I have learned more about 青铜器 than I would have by reading a single textbook?"

- **A: Yes.** The 同型对照墙 + 时空柱 (with event citations) + 段位 reading practice + 待补 suggestions sustain longitudinal learning. After 6 months, I would *use* 青铜器 knowledge in conversation and recognize new pieces I haven't seen before.

- **B: No, but I'd remember stories.** I'd come away with 8-12 vivid narratives (毛公鼎在台北 / 列鼎制度 / 妇好鸮尊 / 曾侯乙编钟) and the **feel** of 三千年. A textbook would have given me more facts; B gives me more emotion. **Trade is real, not strictly worse.**

- **C: No.** I'd have a Pokédex of 30-50 卡片 and a watermarked share image saying I'm 三段. I would not be able to date an unseen bronze, identify a 纹饰, read a 铭文, or explain 列鼎制度. **A 1-hour Wikipedia browse beats 6 months of C.**

---

## Bottom-line verdict

**Motivation winner: v1-A (textual research).** A is the only demo that consistently treats *the user's growing competence* as the reward, not *the user's growing dashboard*. A's hooks survive past Week 2.

**Most dangerous anti-pattern observed: v1-C's stacked "差 N 件解锁 X" panels on me.html and index.html.** This is the team's own forbidden pattern (gamification-mechanics §5.3) shipped three times on the same page. Builder C added a "🌿 不必每天打开" disclaimer at the bottom — the disclaimer is the tell that the designer knew this was wrong and shipped it anyway.

**For v2:** Take A's discipline as foundation. Import B's 礼器归位 mechanic and 流散篇 emotion. Strip C's progress-bar saturation. Keep C's scan reveal animation. Keep B's monologue audio. Forbid any 我的 page from showing more than 2 progress bars simultaneously.
